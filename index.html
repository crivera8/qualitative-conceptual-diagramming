Worth a minute before you start

How this tool works

You'll need to be signed in to Claude to use this tool.

Where your words go

  • Whatever you send Thea or Drew is passed to Claude, Anthropic's AI, to generate a reply — that's the whole mechanism behind both personas.
  • This app doesn't keep a copy anywhere. Your conversation lives in this browser tab only; close it or hit refresh and it's gone for good.
  • Once your message reaches Anthropic's side, it's covered by Anthropic's own privacy policy and terms, not this page.
  • Before pasting in an excerpt, strip out anything that could identify a participant, plus anything confidential or proprietary.

What Anthropic does with it

Anthropic may use the content you send Claude to improve their models — their privacy policy has the full picture. This tool itself doesn't collect or train on anything you write here; past the point it leaves your browser, Anthropic's data practices are what govern it.

To opt out of sharing your inputs and outputs with Anthropic, please open Settings → Privacy in Claude.ai or the Claude mobile app. Under “Help Improve our AI models,” toggle the setting off. This stops new chats and coding sessions from being used for future model training; data already used in training is not removed. Anthropic’s instructions

What the AI gets wrong

  • They're a sounding board, not a stand-in for your advisor, coauthors, or reviewers.
  • They can misread what you've written or reach the wrong conclusion about it.
  • Neither one knows the live debates in your specific corner of the field, or anything unpublished you haven't told them.
  • They can state something wrong with total confidence.
  • Nothing here comes with a promise about quality or publishability. Treat it as a starting point, not a verdict.

A note on bias

Like any AI trained on existing scholarship, Thea and Drew can gravitate toward familiar methods, frameworks, and academic traditions. If your work sits outside that mainstream — non-Western contexts, understudied populations, less conventional methods — weigh their input accordingly, and don't let it substitute for other perspectives.

No one's reading over its shoulder

Every reply is generated automatically, with no person checking it before it reaches you. Verifying what Thea and Drew tell you is on you — nothing either of them says should be taken as final, only as one input to weigh against everything else you know.

Check journal policy

Policies on AI assistance vary by journal and keep shifting — some want disclosure, some restrict certain uses outright. Check your target journal's current policy before this tool touches anything that ends up in your submission; getting that wrong is a risk to the whole paper, not just this figure.

Figure Creation Agent

Thea & Drew

This AI tool is designed to help management researchers with qualitative data develop figures for their research. Thea pushes on your constructs and the relationships between them until there's something worth drawing, then Drew builds it.

Thea

Hi, I'm Thea. I help you think through your ideas and how they relate to each other — I'll take what you've got and push it until it's clear enough to build a figure from.

Drew

And I'm Drew. Once the thinking's in decent shape, just tell me and I'll turn it into an actual figure — boxes, arrows, whatever it needs. No dragging things around yourself; just tell me what to add or change and I'll redraw it, easy.

Thea

Before we get going, tell me a few things about what you're working on.
